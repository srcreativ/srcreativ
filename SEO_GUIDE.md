# SEO operating guide

SR Studio now provides crawlable pages, a dynamic sitemap, a domain-aware
`robots.txt`, Open Graph previews, Twitter previews, and structured data for a
Mumbai wedding photography business.

## Before launch or a domain change

1. Set `VITE_SITE_URL` to the final HTTPS domain at build time, for example
   `https://www.example.in`. This controls canonical URLs and social previews.
2. Add the domain to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. Verify
   ownership with the DNS method where possible.
3. Submit `https://your-domain/sitemap.xml` to both services. The sitemap is
   generated by the Worker, so it always uses the domain visitors reached.
4. Test the homepage with Google Rich Results Test and validate share previews
   in Facebook Sharing Debugger and LinkedIn Post Inspector.
5. Create or claim the Google Business Profile using the exact business name,
   phone number, email, service area, and genuine customer-facing address or
   service-area setting. Do not invent an address or reviews.

## Ongoing optimisation

- Keep the business name, phone, email, and city identical on the website,
  Google Business Profile, Instagram, YouTube, and wedding directories.
- Give every new gallery photo a descriptive title; it becomes useful image
  alternative text on the public portfolio.
- Publish real wedding stories and venue pages only when there is original
  work and client permission. Link them from the portfolio and add each page
  to the sitemap.
- Request reviews from real clients and reply professionally. Do not add
  ratings to structured data unless they are eligible under Google policies.
- Review Search Console monthly for index coverage, mobile usability, Core Web
  Vitals, search queries, and broken pages.

## Built-in endpoints

- `/robots.txt` — allows public pages and excludes `/admin` and `/auth`.
- `/sitemap.xml` — lists public indexable pages.
- `/.well-known/security.txt` — points researchers to the disclosure policy.

Admin and sign-in routes are marked `noindex` and are omitted from the sitemap.
